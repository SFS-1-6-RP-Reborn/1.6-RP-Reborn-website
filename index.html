Rules:

To upgrade comms you need to send a commms satellite there

No unrealistic rocket proportions (for example large fairings, and rockets that shouldn't fly)

You can't randomly launch rockets to pluto if you haven't even gone interplanetary, far interplanetary, or even just going to orbit

BP editing is allowed just don't BP edit abuse. You can use heat on for creative use in the bp editing files but only for stuff that needs clipped engines like custom LES's.

Collaboration is allowed!

Please include a few screenshots of your launches or we can't verify it happened

You can launch a lot/day

Every launch must have a clear purpose (testing, research, mission goals, or progression). Purely random or empty launches are not allowed.

Missions must scale realistically. Small progression steps are required (for example: LEO → HEO → lunar orbit → landing).

Mission feasibility matters. Overly far-fetched missions may be ruled as failures, while realistic step-up missions are more likely to succeed.

Rejected launches aren't strikes but Engine or Roshan can give strikes if it seems widly out of the rules. and if you get 3 strikes your kicked

What's money?

Money is required to build and launch rockets.

You get 2000 money for first manned events.

You get 1000 money for first unmanned events.

You get 250 money for first uncontrollable unmanned events (though if a unmanned probe already did what your uncontrollable craft reached then it dosen't count)

You also get money from contracts, the amount you get payed is determined by the contract

Claims

If you land on a celestial body first you may claim up to 50% ownership of that body. Claim percentage scales with base size and permanence. Trading of territorial claims is allowed. If an agency controls more than 80% of a moon or planet (excluding Phobos, Deimos, and captured asteroids), they may temporarily launch from that body by changing the Space Center settings file. After the launch, the Space Center must be reverted back to Earth.
